Frequently Asked Questions & Tips

How does the bookmarklet detect my store pod?

The moment you click the bookmarklet, it auto-detects both your Primary and Secondary dark store IDs directly from Swiggy's active page session and recent network calls, displaying your Store Pod ID right at the top of the sidebar.

How do I open deals directly in the Swiggy mobile app?

Product links open Swiggy Instamart with the exact product pre-searched. On mobile, if Telegram's or browser's web view opens, tap the menu (⋮) → "Open in Chrome/Browser" (or enable app deep-links) to launch the native Swiggy app immediately!

How do I choose specific subcategories?

Click on any category card to expand its subcategories. Click individual subcategory pills to toggle them on or off, or use the "Select All" / "Clear" buttons.

Why choose fewer subcategories?

Scanning 2 to 4 aisles completes in only 1–2 seconds and completely eliminates 429 WAF rate limit challenges from Swiggy.

How does the brand filter work?

In the results tab, use the "All Brands" dropdown to instantly narrow down items to your favorite brands (e.g. Aashirvaad, Fortune, Amul).
